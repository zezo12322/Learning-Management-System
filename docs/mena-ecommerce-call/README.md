## What this call is

A technical-assistance programme (no grants/funding) for high-potential e-commerce and digital trade platforms legally registered in **Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, or Tunisia**, run under Component 2 ("Operational Tools & Platform Support") of the EU/BMZ-funded programme implemented by GIZ and ITC. Support may include expert advisory, technical upgrades, integrations (payments/logistics), regulatory & compliance guidance (incl. GDPR), digital marketing mentorship, SME-onboarding support, and B2B matchmaking.

**Why we qualify conceptually:** the call explicitly covers platforms that are "product **or service focused**". OptimaTech LMS is a **service-focused digital marketplace**: multiple sellers (instructors and training providers) offer paid courses/learning services to buyers (learners), with product listing (catalog), ordering (enrollment), payment processing (Paymob unified checkout), and order management (payment + enrollment records) — matching the call's platform definition point by point.
